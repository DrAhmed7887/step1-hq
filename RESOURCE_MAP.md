## WHY THIS REPLACES CRAMFIGHTER

CramFighter approach:
- Monday: Dirty Medicine Ethics Part 1 (12m) + Part 2 (10m) + Bootcamp Classical Conditioning...
- Problem: What if you already know Ethics cold but Renal is at 39%?
- Problem: What if you're exhausted and it scheduled 6h 45m of content?
- Problem: You fall behind one day and the whole schedule is broken.
- Result: "I used it for a week, then fell behind. Not worth the money."

Coach approach:
- You open the app. "Energy? Medium. Hours? 4."
- Coach checks: Renal at 39%, untouched 11 days. Ethics at 78%, studied yesterday.
- Coach says: "Skip Ethics today. Attack Renal. Here's your plan:
  1. Pathoma Ch 10 (45m)
  2. FA p.616-635 (60m) 
  3. UWorld 20 Qs Renal tutor (90m)
  4. Anki reviews (30m)"
- Total: 3h 45m. Fits your 4 hours. Targets your weakest system.

The coach doesn't need a calendar. It needs:
1. The resource map above (WHAT exists per system)
2. Your performance data (WHERE you're weak)
3. Your energy and time (HOW MUCH you can do today)

That's it. CramFighter is retired. The coach is your scheduler now.

## FOR ANTIGRAVITY: Implementation

When the coaching engine selects a system for the day, it should:
1. Look up this resource map
2. Check which resources the user has NOT marked as completed
3. Prescribe the NEXT uncompleted resource in order: Pathoma → FA → Sketchy → UWorld
4. Show the specific title and duration (e.g., "Pathoma Ch 10 - Renal, 45 min")
5. After user marks it done, update the resource completion status

This is lighter than CramFighter because:
- No complex scheduling algorithm
- No re-balancing when you fall behind
- The coach just picks the NEXT thing you need based on performance
- If you skip a day, nothing breaks — the coach just adjusts tomorrow
