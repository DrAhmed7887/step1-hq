## THE $100/HOUR TUTOR REPLACEMENT

What does a $100/hr USMLE tutor actually DO?

1. Reviews your UWorld performance → identifies weak systems
   OUR COACH DOES THIS: System Heat Map + accuracy tracking

2. Creates a study schedule → adjusts weekly
   OUR COACH DOES THIS: Periodization + phase management

3. Explains concepts you don't understand
   OUR COACH DOES THIS: AI Quiz + FA Navigator + NotebookLM

4. Holds you accountable → "Did you study today?"
   OUR COACH DOES THIS: Daily check-in + streak + neglect alerts

5. Tells you when you're ready to take the exam
   OUR COACH DOES THIS: NBME predictor + readiness signal

6. Motivates you when you want to quit
   OUR COACH DOES THIS: Contextual stories + personal anchors

7. Knows YOUR specific weaknesses (not generic advice)
   OUR COACH DOES THIS: Tracks every system, every score, every day

The only thing a human tutor has that we don't:
the ability to EXPLAIN a concept in real-time conversation.
But Claude API + NotebookLM + ChatGPT fill that gap.
